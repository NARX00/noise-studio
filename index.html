Free to use and share. Runs entirely on your device — no account, no tracking, no cost, works offline.

This is a comfort and focus tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Sound affects everyone differently; there is no single "correct" choice, so go with whatever feels good to you at a soft volume.

If you are struggling with sleep, focus, anxiety, or ringing in your ears (tinnitus), please reach out to a qualified healthcare professional — this tool is not a substitute for care. If you are in distress, contacting local emergency or crisis services is always okay.
