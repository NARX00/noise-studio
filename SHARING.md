## A note on how you talk about it

Because this tool is aimed partly at people who are struggling, a few things are worth
keeping in the language wherever you share it:

- **Don't promise outcomes.** "Many people find it helpful" — not "this will fix your
  sleep." The evidence for noise and cognition is real but modest, and it varies a lot
  between individuals.
- **Keep the volume advice.** Soft is better, for their hearing and for effectiveness.
- **Always leave the door open to real help.** The line about speaking to a
  professional isn't legal boilerplate — for someone in a bad place, it may be the
  most useful sentence on the page.
- **Say it's free, plainly, and mean it.** People in difficulty are used to "free"
  meaning "free trial." Saying "no account, no ads, no tracking, no cost" removes the
  suspicion that gets in the way of them trying it.
